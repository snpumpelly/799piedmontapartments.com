The Building

Built in 1958,
renewed for now

799 Piedmont has stood in the Garden District since 1958 — the curved corner tower, paneled masonry, and tall windows give it a presence you don't see in most Midtown buildings its age. In 2023, it was taken back to the studs and rebuilt.

What came out of that renovation is unusual: brand-new systems and finishes inside a building that kept its original character. Glass doorknobs and five-panel doors were kept. Crown molding was restored, not replaced. The curved walls of the tower units are exactly where they have always been.

  • In-unit washer & dryer — every home
  • Stainless appliances with gas ranges
  • Granite counters & shaker cabinetry
  • Central HVAC — every home
  • Original millwork & glass hardware
  • Free off-street parking on site
  • Restored hardwood & wide-plank flooring
  • Private entrances on select homes

The Neighborhood

Midtown, on foot

The Garden District sits on the quiet side of Midtown — tree-lined streets and historic homes — while everything Midtown is known for stays within a short walk. You can leave the car parked.

Piedmont Park

Atlanta's front lawn — 200 acres of green with the Active Oval, the Saturday farmers market, and the Botanical Garden at its north end. Straight up Piedmont.

0.9 miles · 17 minute walk

BeltLine Eastside Trail

Three paved miles connecting Ponce City Market, Krog Street Market, and Reynoldstown — easiest access east via Ponce City Market.

About 1 mile

MARTA Rail

North Avenue and Midtown stations both sit a short walk west on the Red and Gold lines — Downtown, Buckhead, and a direct ride to the airport, no transfer.

North Avenue Station · under 1 mile

Emory University Hospital Midtown

Walk to work. One of the largest employers in the neighborhood, and close enough to come home between shifts.

0.7 miles

Georgia Tech

West across Midtown, with the campus, research corridor, and Tech Square startup district a short ride or bike away.

1.5 miles

Arts & Culture

The Fox Theatre, the High Museum, Alliance Theatre, and the Woodruff Arts Center all sit along the Peachtree corridor.

Frequently asked

The most common questions we get before a tour. Don't see yours? Ask through the form above.

How close is 799 Piedmont to Piedmont Park?

About 0.9 miles, roughly a 17-minute walk up Piedmont Ave. The block itself scores 94 on Walk Score — a Walker's Paradise — so groceries, restaurants, and MARTA are all closer than the park.

Is parking included?

Yes — free off-street parking on site for residents, at no additional cost.

Is there laundry in the unit?

Every home has its own in-unit washer and dryer. There's no shared laundry room to schedule around.

How old is the building?

799 Piedmont has stood in the Garden District since 1958. It was taken back to the studs and fully renovated in 2023 — new systems and finishes inside, original character kept intact.

What unit types are available?

Eleven homes total: one studio (500 sq ft), nine one-bedrooms (704 sq ft), and one two-bedroom (1,133 sq ft). See the availability table for current pricing and what's open now.

How do I schedule a tour or apply?

Use the form above — choose "Schedule a tour," "Apply now," "Join the waitlist," or "I'm interested, let's talk," whichever fits. We reply the same day where we can, and tours run seven days a week by appointment.

What's the best way to reach you?

The contact form is fastest. We don't list a phone number — every inquiry through the form gets a personal reply, usually the same day.

Is there a grocery store nearby?

Publix is about a 4-minute walk south on Piedmont Ave — a full-size store, so the weekly shop doesn't require a car.
